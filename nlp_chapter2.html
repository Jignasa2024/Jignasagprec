Bag of Words (BoW)

The Bag of Words model is one of the simplest and most widely used techniques to convert textual data into a numerical representation...

Example:

Let’s say we have two documents:

  • Doc1: “NLP is fun”
  • Doc2: “NLP is powerful”
Word Doc1 Doc2
NLP 1 1
is 1 1
fun 1 0
powerful 0 1
